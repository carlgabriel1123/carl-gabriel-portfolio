What I do

Four growth levers. One operator.

Paid media, e-commerce, creators and AI automation. Most growing brands split those four across four vendors. I run them as one system, today for three brands in the Philippines and for The Loaded Teashop's creator ad program at AMA Holdings, the US owner of The Loaded Teashop and Goli Nutrition.

Paid media

Meta ads that scale what wins

Media buying, structured creative testing and retargeting. ROAS, CPA and funnel analysis guide every budget move, and Pixel + Conversions API tracking keeps delivery honest. Built for Shopify and DTC brands on Facebook and Instagram.

E-commerce

Shopify stores built to convert

Store setup, theme customization, landing pages, app integrations and CRO. Plus marketplace launches and sale events on TikTok Shop, Shopee and Lazada.

Social & creators

Creator content that feeds the ads

UGC strategy and briefs, creator recruitment, short-form editing and influencer & affiliate programs across TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Pinterest. So your UGC creative strategist and your media buyer are the same person.

AI & search

Automation that does the busywork

n8n workflows (more than twenty live for one client), dashboards and knowledge bots on Supabase, Vercel and the Claude API, and an AI SEO agent that audits and fixes stores through the Shopify Admin API. Plus SEO, GEO (generative engine optimization) and AEO (answer engine optimization), so your brand shows up in search and in AI answers.
