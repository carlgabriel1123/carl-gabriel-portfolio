Straight answers

What does a Meta ads specialist actually do?

A Meta ads specialist plans, buys and tests ads on Facebook and Instagram, then reads the data to decide where the next peso or dollar goes. In my case the job also covers making the creative, fixing tracking with the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, and fixing the Shopify store your ads send traffic to.

What's the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in Google. AEO, answer engine optimization, shapes a page so Google or an assistant can lift a direct answer from it. GEO, generative engine optimization, gives ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews enough clear facts to name your brand as the source. I handle all three with an AI SEO agent I run on Shopify stores.
