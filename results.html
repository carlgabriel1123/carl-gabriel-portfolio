ROAS, plainly

What is a good ROAS for Meta ads?

It depends on your margin. Break-even ROAS is 1 divided by your gross margin, so a product with a 50% margin breaks even at 2× and a 25% margin needs 4×. Anything above that is profit after ad spend. When someone quotes a ROAS, ask whether it is one campaign's peak or the blended figure across everything they spent.
