Before you book

The questions founders ask first.

With an agency, your strategist, media buyer, creative team and store developer are different people — and feedback dies between them. I run the whole loop: the creative, the ads, the data and the store. Every signal from Ads Manager shapes the next creative, the next budget and the next store fix the same week. One brain, one feedback loop.

Access to your Shopify store and Meta ad account, your current offers and margins, and any brand assets you already have. If you sell on TikTok Shop, Shopee or Lazada, seller-centre access too. If you've run ads before, I'll want to see what's been tested. We cover all of it on the first call.

The first sprint fixes tracking first — Meta Pixel and Conversions API — so nothing launches blind. In parallel I produce the first batch of short-form ads and statics around the angles we agree on. Campaigns go live with structured tests as soon as that batch is ready, usually within the first week or two.

I work best with Shopify and marketplace brands that already have a product selling and want a tighter creative-and-ads engine behind it. I've run growth for wellness supplements, baby care, sports nutrition, nasal strips and automotive brands across the Philippines, Singapore and the USA. Not sure you're a fit? Book the call and I'll tell you straight.

Every engagement starts with a strategy call — no pitch deck. From there I scope the work to your stage and goals, so pricing reflects what you actually need. You'll know exactly what you're paying for before we start.
