Why me

Agencies play a telephone game. You get one brain.

Same budget, fewer hand-offs — one person who makes the creative, runs the ads, reads the data and fixes the store, all in one feedback loop.

When your strategist, your media buyer, your creative team and your Shopify developer are four different vendors, feedback dies in a Slack thread. The ad account says hook #3 is winning; the next batch of creative never hears about it. The product page leaks half the traffic; nobody's job is to notice.

I've sat inside that machine — managing 300 UGC creators and brand operations for Goli Nutrition inside a large-scale creator and performance-marketing ecosystem, working across Meta tools, analytics dashboards and content systems every day.

Now I run the whole loop myself. Every metric I see in Ads Manager shapes the next move — in the funnel, the budget, the creative and the store.
